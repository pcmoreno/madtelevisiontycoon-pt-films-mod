[size=5][b]Bring Portuguese cinema to your channel.[/b][/size]

The [b]Portuguese Film Pack[/b] ("Pacote Português", the name you'll see in the installer) adds 80 Portuguese films released between 1981 and 2005 to Mad Television Tycoon. You'll find slow-burning art-house dramas, festival favourites, period epics, crime thrillers and the comedies that filled Portuguese cinemas, all made to fit the game:

[list]
[*]80 new films that turn up at the film market, the rental shop and the pirate dealer, and with your rivals
[*]Parody titles and names, the way the game does its own films: "Argument Over", "The Liquid in the Veins", "Zone K", directed by "Manoel de Olivera" and "Joaquim Leirão"
[*]80 new black-and-white pictures in the style of the game's own
[*]An original one-line synopsis for every film
[*]Ratings and audience potential based on awards and real ticket sales where known, so the arthouse classics score high with critics but draw small audiences, and the hits draw crowds
[*]Release years, running times (2-4 blocks), age ratings and genres for every film
[*]Country: Portugal
[*]Portuguese players: switch the game to Portuguese to see the titles in Portuguese
[*]194 directors and actors, credited on the films
[/list]

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Close the game.
[*]Download the zip. Optional, to avoid Windows' security prompts: right-click the zip > Properties > tick "Unblock" > OK.
[*]Right-click the zip > Extract All. Don't run anything from inside the zip.
[*]Open the extracted folder and double-click [b]Install.bat[/b].
[list]
[*]It finds the game through Steam. If it can't, it asks for the game folder (Steam: right-click the game > Manage > Browse local files).
[*]Windows may say "The publisher could not be verified". Click Run. Batch files can't carry a publisher signature; Install.bat and install.ps1 are plain text, so you can open them in Notepad and read what they do.
[*]If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".
[*]Steam installs games under Program Files, which is write-protected, so the installer will usually ask for administrator rights. Click Yes.
[/list]
[*]Start a [b]new[/b] game.
[/list]
No Python or other software needed: the installer uses Windows PowerShell, which comes with Windows.
Mod managers (Vortex etc.) aren't supported: use Install.bat.

[size=4][b]After a game update[/b][/size]
Run Install.bat again. Updates can add films and pictures of their own, and the mod's files need to take them in. Keep the extracted folder for this.

[size=4][b]Uninstall[/b][/size]
Double-click [b]Uninstall.bat[/b]. It removes only the files the installer wrote and that are still unchanged. The game is then back to its own films.

[size=4][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Tested with game version EA2026-10-15A.
[*][b]Conflicts:[/b] can't be combined with other mods that replace the game's film database (DATABASE\MOD\ItemsData.json and CastData.json), such as Real Names or the Polish database. The game loads only one of these files, so only one such mod can be active. The installer refuses to overwrite another mod's files.
[*]Existing saves: start a new game to be sure everything shows up.
[/list]

[size=4][b]What it changes[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]EXTERN\DATABASE\MOD\ItemsData.json and CastData.json: your game's own films and cast, plus this mod's entries
[*]EXTERN\DATABASE\MOD\pt_films_install.json: the install record (every file written, with a fingerprint)
[*]EXTERN\SCRN\<GENRE>\: 80 pictures, numbered after the game's own
[/list]
No game files are replaced, and the download contains no game data.

[size=4][b]About the pictures (AI-generated content)[/b][/size]
The pictures are AI-generated lookalikes, each based on a still from its film: 60 made from a written description of the still and 20 by restyling the still itself. They're turned black and white to match the game and show made-up people in place of the films' actors.

[size=4][b]For modders[/b][/size]
Film IDs 9000001-9000080, cast IDs 9000001-9000194. Pictures go after the vanilla ones in each genre folder, and the installer stops if the numbering has gaps.

[size=4][b]Credits[/b][/size]
Data researched from IMDb, Portuguese and English Wikipedia, ICA (Instituto do Cinema e Audiovisual) box-office records, and Cinecartaz, CinePT and TVCine for age ratings. Synopses are original.
